# 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Fund – Community Contribution Management App

Family Fund is an Android application designed to help families and small communities manage and maintain monthly contribution records in one place.

The application allows community members to submit their monthly contribution payment details, while administrators can review, verify, and update payment records.

The main goal is to make community funding transparent, organized, and easy to manage, especially for supporting family and community members with educational fees, business needs, and other financial requirements.

## ✨ Features

- **Two User Roles:** Separate Admin and Member modes with role-based functionality.
- **Monthly Contribution Tracking:** Maintain fixed monthly contribution records for each member.
- **Payment Submission:** Members can submit payment details for monthly contributions.
- **Admin Verification:** Admins receive payment requests and can approve or reject them.
- **Payment History:** Track who has paid and who has not paid for each month.
- **Member Management:** Maintain community member information and contribution records.
- **Loan Management:** Maintain records of financial assistance given to community members, including repayment details.
- **Real-time Data Updates:** Keep records synchronized across multiple users.
- **Loading & Error Handling:** Handle loading, success, and error states across different screens.
- **Shimmer UI:** Provide skeleton loading effects for a better user experience.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI Toolkit:** Jetpack Compose
- **Design System:** Material 3
- **Architecture:** MVVM
- **Backend:** Firebase Realtime Database
- **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication
- **Asynchronous Programming:** Kotlin Coroutines & Flow
- **State Management:** StateFlow
- **Dependency Injection:** Hilt / Dagger
- **IDE:** Android Studio

## 🔐 User Roles

### 👨‍💼 Admin Mode

- View all community members.
- Monitor monthly contribution records.
- Review submitted payment requests.
- Approve or reject payment submissions.
- Maintain member payment history.
- Manage community funding and assistance records.

### 👤 Member Mode

- View personal profile and contribution details.
- Check monthly payment status.
- Submit payment details for verification.
- View payment history.
- Check community funding and loan records.
- Track repayment information.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Application

Family Fund is designed to simplify financial record management within a family or community.

It can help community members support each other in different situations, such as:

- Educational and college fees.
- Small business requirements.
- Emergency financial assistance.
- Other family and community needs.

The application focuses on transparency, accountability, and organized financial record management.

## 🧠 Technical Challenges & Learning

During development, I am focusing on:

- Managing data for multiple users.
- Handling real-time database updates.
- Maintaining consistent records across Admin and Member interfaces.
- Implementing role-based access and functionality.
- Managing separate UI states for different screens.
- Handling loading, success, and error states.
- Implementing shimmer effects for better loading experiences.
- Maintaining clean and scalable application architecture.

One of the biggest challenges is managing multiple users and keeping their records consistent when data is updated or changed by different users.
